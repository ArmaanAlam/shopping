ShopVoice listens to your voice commands and automatically updates your shopping list. Here are some templates of what you can say:

1. Adding Items

"Add milk"

"Buy 3 packages of chocolate cookies"

"I need 2 bottles of organic water"

2. Removing Items

"Remove milk"

"Delete apples from my list"

3. Search & Pricing

"Find organic peanut butter"

"Search toothpaste under 5 dollars"

"Show me pasta under 10 dollars"

4. Assistant Queries

"Read my shopping list"

"Clear my shopping list"

The app automatically categorizes items (Dairy, Produce, Bakery, Pantry, Snacks, etc.) and tracks quantities using natural language pattern matching.
